🚨 In Case of Life-Threatening Emergency

Severe bleeding, snakebite, unconsciousness, or acute breathing difficulty:

📞 Call 108 (India Emergency Ambulance)

🇬🇧 Quick Offline Emergency Steps

  • Severe Bleeding: Firm direct palm pressure with clean cloth for 10-15 mins continuously. Elevate limb.
  • Burns: Hold under cool running tap water for 20 mins. Never apply ice or toothpaste.
  • Snakebite: Keep victim 100% still. Never cut or suck venom. Rush for Anti-Snake Venom (ASV).
  • Unconscious: Check breathing. If breathing, place in recovery position on side. If not, start CPR 30 compressions.
